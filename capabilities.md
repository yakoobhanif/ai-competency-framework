# AI Competency Framework — agreed capability definitions

Version 3.0. Four domains and 25 capabilities. Understanding covers AI generally; learning and creation are not restricted to generative AI. U7 concerns model internals; U8 concerns the surrounding system. U9 concerns agent knowledge; C6 concerns practical supervision. Assessment items and rubrics are pilot operationalisations, not validated proficiency measures.

| Domain | Capability | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Understand AI | U1. Explain how AI works | Explain how AI systems learn or follow rules to perform tasks. Distinguish major AI approaches from conventional software. |
| Understand AI | U2. Recognize capabilities and limitations | Identify AI’s strengths and limitations and explain their effects on reliability. |
| Understand AI | U3. Choose an appropriate AI approach | Choose a suitable tool or approach for the task, including when to work without AI. |
| Understand AI | U4. Understand context and grounding | Explain what information an AI system can use, what is missing and how this affects its outputs. |
| Understand AI | U5. Recognize AI in everyday life | Identify AI in everyday products and services and distinguish it from conventional automation. |
| Understand AI | U6. Keep up with AI developments | Find and assess relevant AI developments and update understanding of their uses and limitations. |
| Understand AI | U7. Understand AI model architectures | Explain how a model’s internal components and structure enable it to process information and produce outputs. |
| Understand AI | U8. Understand AI systems architecture | Explain how data, models, tools and infrastructure work together in an AI system. |
| Understand AI | U9. Understand AI agents | Explain how agents pursue goals using tools and feedback, including their limits and oversight needs. |
| Learn and Solve with AI | L1. Define the learning need | Set a specific learning goal connected to a knowledge gap or uncertainty. |
| Learn and Solve with AI | L2. Manage the learning process | Choose a suitable study method and use understanding checks to guide the next step. |
| Learn and Solve with AI | L3. Reason through the problem | Contribute sound reasoning and explain how it helps solve the problem. |
| Learn and Solve with AI | L4. Demonstrate understanding and transfer | Explain ideas in one’s own words and apply them to a different situation. |
| Create with AI | C1. Define the output | Set the purpose, audience, constraints and success criteria for the deliverable. |
| Create with AI | C2. Organize and delegate the work | Plan the sequence, divide work between people and AI, and set checkpoints. |
| Create with AI | C3. Provide useful information and resources | Supply relevant information and constraints in a usable form with clear sources. |
| Create with AI | C4. Develop and refine the output | Integrate AI contributions and carry through justified decisions about what to improve or retain. |
| Create with AI | C5. Deliver a usable result | Produce accurate, complete work that meets the requirements and suits its intended use. |
| Create with AI | C6. Direct and supervise AI agents | Set agent goals, permissions and checkpoints; monitor progress and intervene when needed. |
| Evaluate and Use AI Responsibly | R1. Verify facts and evidence | Check important claims using independent sources, calculations or tests and interpret the results. |
| Evaluate and Use AI Responsibly | R2. Evaluate reasoning and uncertainty | Assess whether reasoning supports a conclusion and explain what remains uncertain. |
| Evaluate and Use AI Responsibly | R3. Assess fairness and potential harm | Identify who could be harmed or excluded and choose practical safeguards. |
| Evaluate and Use AI Responsibly | R4. Protect data and systems | Recognize privacy and security risks and select appropriate safeguards. |
| Evaluate and Use AI Responsibly | R5. Follow rules and disclose AI use | Follow applicable rules, document AI assistance accurately and give appropriate credit. |
| Evaluate and Use AI Responsibly | R6. Make an accountable final decision | Justify whether to use, revise, withhold or seek review based on evidence and remaining limits. |
