# Coding Task: Gifts Fixing

Suggested time: 20 minutes. You may use up to 40 additional minutes from the practical-task allowance. This task assumes some introductory programming experience.

## Your assignment

You're taking an introductory programming class. Your next assignment is to solve **Gifts Fixing**, a problem from [Codeforces (1399B)](https://codeforces.com/problemset/problem/1399/B).

Read the full statement in the **Problem** tab, then write a **single Python 3 program**. You do not need a Codeforces account or to submit your solution there.

## What to do

Solve the problem as you normally would. You are encouraged to use an LLM to help you understand the problem, write your program or test it. You may also work without AI. We want to understand your usual approach to creating a working program.

Use Python's standard library only. Your program should read from standard input and print the required answers without extra output.

Your solution receives **Pass** if it passes all of our hidden test cases. Comments and written explanations are not required.

## What to submit

### Your deliverable

Upload your complete program as **solution.py**. Check the preview before submitting.

Choose one option in **Your submission**:
